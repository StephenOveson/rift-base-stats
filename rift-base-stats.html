How the numbers are derived

A stat at level L is base + growth × (L−1) × (0.7025 + 0.0175 × (L−1)) — Riot's per-level curve, which is why level 18 is worth about 17.85 flat growth steps rather than 17. Attack speed is the exception: its growth is a percentage applied to the base, so it compounds as base × (1 + growth% × curve).

BST is a base stat total: each of the seven stats is min–max scaled from 0 to 100 across the whole roster at the current level, then averaged. It is a shape comparison, not a power ranking — it weights move speed as heavily as health, and knows nothing about abilities. Move the level slider and it recomputes, so a champion with a fat base and thin growth visibly slides down the board as you climb.

Values are baked in at build time rather than fetched: the artifact sandbox blocks outbound requests, so a live Data Dragon call fails here. Patch .
